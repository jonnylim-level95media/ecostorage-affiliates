## 1. Definitions

- **"Affiliate"** — an individual or business approved by EcoStorage to join
  the Programme.
- **"Referral Link"** — the unique URL issued to an Affiliate.
- **"Referral Code"** — the unique promo code issued to an Affiliate.
- **"Referred Customer"** — a new EcoStorage customer attributed to an
  Affiliate under Section 3.
- **"Qualifying Period"** — 60 consecutive days starting from the Referred
  Customer's storage start date, or, if the Referred Customer's storage
  ends earlier, the period up to their successful move-out from the
  warehouse.
- **"Qualified Referral"** — a Referred Customer who completes the Qualifying
  Period in Good Standing (Section 5).

## 2. Joining the Programme

2.1 Anyone may apply via the online application form. Applications are
reviewed manually; EcoStorage may approve or decline any application at its
sole discretion, without giving reasons.

2.2 Affiliates must be at least 18 years old and provide accurate contact and
payout details.

## 3. Attribution — when a customer counts as your referral

3.1 A new customer is attributed to an Affiliate only if **either**:
  (a) they submit an enquiry within **14 days** of clicking that Affiliate's
  Referral Link, on the same browser/device; **or**
  (b) they enter that Affiliate's Referral Code on the enquiry/sign-up form.

3.2 If both apply to different Affiliates, the **Referral Code entered by
the customer takes priority** over a Referral Link.

3.3 Customers who do not meet 3.1 are **not attributed to any Affiliate**,
and no commission is payable on them. This includes enquiries made more than
14 days after clicking a link without the Referral Code being entered, and
cases where tracking was blocked or cleared by the customer's browser.

3.4 Each customer can be attributed to one Affiliate only, once. Existing or
former EcoStorage customers, and customers already attributed to another
Affiliate, are not eligible.

3.5 EcoStorage's records are final in determining attribution.

## 4. Referred Customer Offer

4.1 Referred Customers receive **[1 month free storage]** on a minimum
**[4-month]** commitment. The offer applies to storage fees only; the standard admin fee and
security deposit apply to every customer in full and are not discounted.

4.2 The offer cannot be combined with other promotions unless stated, and is
subject to EcoStorage's standard customer terms.

## 5. Qualifying Period & Good Standing

5.1 Commission is **held** and becomes payable only once the Referred
Customer completes the Qualifying Period in **Good Standing**:
  (a) **standard case** — 60 days from the storage start date; or
  (b) **storage of less than 60 days** — upon the Referred Customer's
  **successful outbound** (all items moved out of the warehouse, signed
  off, and all amounts paid in full).

5.2 **Good Standing** means that throughout the Qualifying Period the Referred
Customer:
  (a) has paid all amounts due (including any admin fee and security deposit)
  **on or before the due date** — any payment received more than
  **3** days late counts as a late payment;
  (b) has not defaulted on, or had to be sent to collections for, any amount;
  (c) has not cancelled, terminated or requested a refund of their contract;
  and
  (d) has not had their account suspended for breach of EcoStorage's customer
  terms.

5.3 If the Referred Customer fails Good Standing at any point in the
Qualifying Period, the commission for that referral is **forfeited** and will
not become payable later.

## 6. Commission

6.1 Commission is earned per Qualified Referral at the Affiliate's tier rate
**at the time the referral qualifies**:

| Tier | Qualified Referrals (rolling 12 months) | Commission |
|---|---|---|
| Starter | 1–10 | 10% of the customer's first full month's net rent |
| Partner | 11–30 | 20% |
| Elite | 31+ | 35% |

The Affiliate's tier is set by the number of Qualified Referrals in the 12
months before the referral qualifies, including that referral (so the 11th
Qualified Referral is paid at Partner rate).

6.2 "Net rent" excludes admin fees, security deposits, GST and any free or
discounted months.

6.3 Tier upgrades apply to referrals qualifying after the upgrade; they are
not applied retroactively.

6.4 Commission is paid **30 days after the referral qualifies** under 5.1
(i.e. 30 days after the 60-day Qualifying Period ends, or 30 days after a
short-term customer's successful outbound), via **[PayNow / bank transfer]**.
If the Referred Customer defaults on any amount relating to the Qualifying
Period before payout, the commission is forfeited.

6.5 Affiliates are responsible for any tax on commissions they receive.

## 7. Prohibited Conduct

Affiliates must not:
  (a) refer themselves, members of their household, or accounts they control;
  (b) submit fake, duplicate or incentivised-but-unintended enquiries;
  (c) publish Referral Codes on coupon, voucher, deal or cashback sites, or
  in browser-extension databases;
  (d) bid on "EcoStorage" or related brand terms in paid search ads;
  (e) send unsolicited messages (spam) or contact numbers on the Do Not Call
  Registry in breach of the PDPA;
  (f) misrepresent EcoStorage's prices, services or offers, or imply they are
  an EcoStorage employee.

Breach may result in forfeiture of unpaid commission, clawback of paid
commission obtained through the breach, deactivation of Referral Codes, and
termination.

## 8. Disclosure

Affiliates must clearly disclose that they may earn a commission when sharing
Referral Links or Codes publicly (e.g. "#affiliate" or "I earn a referral
fee"), in line with ASAS guidelines.

9.3 EcoStorage processes personal data in accordance with the PDPA and its

## 10. Changes & Termination

10.1 EcoStorage may change these terms, the customer offer or commission
rates with **[14 days']** notice by email. Commission already earned is not
affected.

10.2 Either party may end participation at any time. Referrals that qualify
after termination are not payable, unless terminated by EcoStorage without
cause, in which case referrals already in their Qualifying Period remain
eligible.
